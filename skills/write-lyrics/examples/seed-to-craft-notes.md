# Example — seed → structure → craft notes

This is a **process** example, not a song to copy. It shows the decision trail an agent should narrate briefly.

## Input seeds (from the human)

- Image: unpaid parking ticket under the wiper after a funeral reception
- Half-line: "everybody brought a casserole and nobody said his name"
- Ten minutes before: sitting in the church lot with the engine off because the radio was playing something too cheerful
- Not about: generic grief / "you're in a better place"

## Phase 2 — Seed picked

**Seed:** "everybody brought a casserole and nobody said his name" (chorus candidate — concrete, slightly bitter, singable).

## Phase 3–3b

- **Structure:** K (verse–verse–chorus) — verses carry the room details; chorus is the only place the name-absence lands.
- **Rhyme:** ABCB verses; chorus AABA with slant on the turn.
- **Avoided:** Structure A (last song used it); down/sound/ground shelf; "broken heart."

## Craft notes (what you'd post after emitting lyrics)

> Seed is the casserole/name line — kept it as an unchanged chorus. Structure K so the verses stay observational and the chorus is the only thesis. ABCB with slant rhymes (lot/off, dish/said). Cut a bridge that explained the grief out loud; the unpaid ticket in verse 2 does that job. No vowel map this round.

## What "good" looks like in the lyric body

- Named objects: Pyrex, folding chair, bulletin with the wrong middle initial
- Time/place anchors: Tuesday reception, gravel lot, choir robe zipper
- No "under the stars," no "hold me tight," no cosmic stack
